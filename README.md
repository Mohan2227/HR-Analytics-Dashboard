# 👨‍💼 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition and workforce trends using Excel and Power BI.

The objective is to identify key factors affecting employee attrition and provide business insights to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Excel
- Power BI
- Pivot Tables
- DAX

---

## 📊 Dataset Information

- Total Employees: 5000
- Departments: Sales, Human Resources, Research & Development
- Multiple Job Roles
- Monthly Salary Information
- Job Satisfaction Ratings
- Employee Attrition Status (Yes / No)

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Employees
- Attrition Employees
- Attrition Rate %
- Average Salary
- Average Age

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Department-wise Attrition

- Analyzed attrition trends across departments.
- Identified departments with the highest employee turnover.

### 2. Gender Distribution

- Compared workforce composition by gender.
- Analyzed employee distribution across the organization.

### 3. Job Role Analysis

- Identified job roles with the highest attrition.
- Compared employee retention across different job roles.

### 4. Salary Analysis

- Evaluated average salary across departments.
- Analyzed the relationship between salary and employee attrition.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Improve employee engagement in high attrition departments.
- Implement retention strategies for critical job roles.
- Enhance employee satisfaction and career growth opportunities.
- Optimize compensation and benefits programs.
